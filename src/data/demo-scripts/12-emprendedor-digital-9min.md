**Pantalla:** Dashboard con MRR Stripe, gastos en USD/CLP, calendario fiscal SII.

**ES:** "Tomás construyó un SaaS para agencias inmobiliarias. Cobra $4.800 USD al mes vía Stripe, opera como SpA en Chile, y trabaja desde su living. Todo su stack es internacional: AWS, Vercel, Linear, Notion, Loom. Antes calculaba sus impuestos en Excel cada noche. Hoy EvoFinz lo hace solo."

**EN:** "Tomas built a SaaS for real-estate agencies. He bills $4,800 USD/month via Stripe, runs a Chilean SpA, and works from his living room. His stack is global: AWS, Vercel, Linear, Notion, Loom. He used to calculate taxes nightly in Excel. EvoFinz now does it for him."

## Escena 2 — Ingresos USD → CLP automáticos (1:00 – 2:30)

**Pantalla:** Stripe import con conversión a CLP usando tasa del día.

**ES:** "Cada cobro de Stripe entra como ingreso en USD, y EvoFinz lo convierte a CLP usando la tasa observada del día para efectos del SII. Mantiene el monto original en USD para la conciliación con Stripe, y el monto convertido en CLP para la declaración. Sin errores, sin doble registro."

**EN:** "Each Stripe charge comes in as USD income, and EvoFinz converts to CLP at the day's official rate for SII reporting. Keeps the USD amount for Stripe reconciliation and CLP for tax filing. No errors, no double entry."

**EN:** "The detector found 14 active international subscriptions, all USD. Monthly total: $312 USD, ~$295k CLP. Shows which are deductible as SpA operating expenses, which to consolidate to annual plans, and which seem unused by usage patterns. Projected savings: $740 USD/year."

## Escena 4 — Análisis de contratos (4:30 – 6:00)

**EN:** "Tomas uploads his AWS Reserved Instance contract. Smart extraction pulls: upfront amount, savings vs on-demand, expiry, renewal policy. Auto-added to fiscal calendar with 60-day alert. Same with his Stripe Terms: fees, payout dates, fund retention."
